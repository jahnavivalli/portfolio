# Akula Jahnavi Valli

### Writer | Aspiring Screenwriter & Filmmaker

I am currently a student at the School of Digital Media and Communication at Mahindra University, with a passion for storytelling that began when I was ten years old.

The reason I started writing was because I didn't like the ending of a book I had read as a child and decided to write my own. Since then, writing has grown from a hobby into something I want to pursue professionally.

I have recently branched into screenwriting, with an interest in dialogue- and emotion-driven stories. I am also currently  exploring video editing and visual design.

## 🎬 Screenwriting

A collection of my original screenwriting work, exploring themes of friendship, loss, grief, relationships, and the choices that shape them.

### [Absence Film](scripts/Absence%20Film.pdf)

A girl tries to deal with the absence of her close friend following a sudden, tragic death.

### [Room 507](scripts/Room%20507.pdf)

After accidentally opening a forbidden room, a girl finds herself followed by the spirit residing within it. Unaware that she is a spirit, she becomes friends with her. That is, until the spirit becomes jealous of the other relationships in her life and tries to end them, by taking her life.

### [Stranded on a Lifeboat](scripts/Stranded%20on%20a%20Lifeboat.pdf)

Two survivors are left stranded on a lifeboat. While one desperately wants to survive and return to the life waiting for them, the other has given up. The story follows their struggle to convince each other to hold on for another day.

### [Time Loop Film Script](scripts/Time%20Loop%20Film%20Script.pdf)

An academically driven girl takes her closest friendship for granted and is on the verge of losing her. But due to a miracle, she is given a final chance to make things right. She finds herself stuck in a time loop, reliving the day before her close friend dies. During the loop, she reconnects with her friend and their relationship grows stronger. But she realises some things are inevitable.
