A Little About Me

I am currently a student at the School of Digital Media and Communication at Mahindra University, aspiring to become a screenwriter and filmmaker.

It all began when I read a book at the age of 10, and hated the ending. I started writing then and h ave been writing to date.

Since then, writing has branches into various styles and genres like poetic writing, horror, romance and tragedy. I haven't figured out what kind of storyteller I want to become, and I haven't stopped exploring. But for now, I am interested in experimenting with everything.

Alongside writing, I am also exploring video editing and visual design.
