04 — ABOUT ME

I've been writing since
I was ten.

I am currently a student at the School of Digital Media and Communication at Mahindra University, aspiring to become a screenwriter and filmmaker.

I started writing when I was ten years old, after reading a book whose ending I didn't like and deciding to write my own.

Since then, writing has grown into a serious creative pursuit. I have recently branched into screenwriting, particularly dialogue- and emotion-driven storytelling.

Alongside writing, I am exploring video editing and visual design as ways to expand the way I tell stories.

Let's tell stories.
